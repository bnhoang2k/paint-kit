## Mechanism

The zipper pull is a scrub handle. The V-shaped gap ends at the pull, so the opening follows the finger. A short
pull springs back shut; a full pull commits, the denim halves slide off sideways and the screen behind builds its
header (shelf report, "mechanism"). The shelf's "missed" note adds that the gap is pinned to the pull and the title
types in letter by letter while the logo de-blurs.

## Beats (Source: frames, shelf report)

- 0.0 to 0.6 s: closed denim, pull at the top.
- 0.7 to 1.1 s: the pull is dragged about a third down; blue shows in a V that ends at the pull.
- About 1.2 to 1.3 s: released; the pull snaps back to the top and the gap closes.
- 1.7 to 2.95 s: pulled again, all the way down; the V widens and the fabric edges bow outward.
- About 3.0 to 3.05 s: the halves slide off the left and right edges.
- 3.1 to 3.85 s: the logo de-blurs and scales up, the title types in, a picture fills the logo.

## What is asked of the visitor

Nothing is explained on screen. The pull is the only thing that looks like a handle; the visitor drags it.

## The surface (what paint-kit has to paint), Source: frames

- The cloth fills the phone screen: a dark slate-blue denim, mottled, with soft vertical folds a few tens of px
  wide. The weave is fine, close to the pixel at phone size; it reads as a heathered texture more than as ribs.
- The zipper runs down the middle: a black tape about an eighth of the screen wide, a silver chain of interlocking
  teeth on it, and a thin light stitch line on each side of the tape.
- The pull is grey metal: a slider body and a hanging tab with a ring. It catches light on its rounded faces; it is
  the brightest, most curved thing on the screen. The teeth catch a small highlight each.
- At rest a sliver of blue shows at the very top, beside the camera island, where the two halves meet.
- While open, the edges of the two halves bow outward in a smooth curve, and the teeth and tape ride on the edge,
  tilted with it. The status bar text (white) stays readable over the denim; the cloth under it is soft (bar 13
  blurs the top 52 px).
- The light does not move in the clip. Light is fixed, from the upper left by the side the teeth shine on.

## What carries the light law

The highlight law needs a convex carrier, not a straight uniform band (Task 7 ruling): the pull's slider body and
tab are domed, and each tooth is a small rounded bar, so their highlights move with the key light. The denim carries
the relief law through its folds (macro relief that stays at detail 0) and the raised tape and teeth.
