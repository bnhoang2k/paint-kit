# Study: 11 Heat tint

No single reference (`shelfKey` none). The page answers the Phase 2 spec's heated-metal bar (Wave 3) from the temper
colour sources.

## What the surface is

Polished carbon steel that has been heated. A thin layer of iron oxide grows on its face, thicker where the steel got
hotter. Light reflects off the oxide's top and off the steel under it, and the two reflections interfere: the colour
that is left is the temper colour. It is thin-film interference, not a glow: tempering heat is far below the
temperature at which steel glows visibly, so the colour stays after the steel cools.

## The temper colours (sources)

- Order with temperature (Wikipedia, "Tempering (metallurgy)", via `paint-kitBrain/Research/2026-11-14-iridescence-sources/1-thin-film.md`):
  faint yellow 176 C, light straw 205 C, dark straw 226 C, brown 260 C, purple 282 C, dark blue 310 C, light blue
  337 C, grey-blue 371 C and over. The colours also depend on time at heat, the alloy and the heat source.
- Thickness (Fujimura 1972, the owner's pick, recorded in the plan's ledger): straw 26 nm, purple 38 nm, blue 54 nm of
  oxide. No source gives a brown thickness.
- The oxide's index: 2.343, Querry's bulk Fe3O4 (the driver's ruling: the only measured source opened). Iron's n and k under it: Johnson and Christy 1974,
  already in the engine (`materials.film`, base `"metal"`).

## Mechanism the page copies

- A piece heated at one end: the oxide is thickest at the hot end and thins away from it, so the bands run blue,
  purple, brown, straw from the hot end toward the cold end, in that order.
- The colour is the highlight's colour: on polished steel, the colour shows where the steel reflects the light. Under
  one key light it shows in the highlight.
- Turning the light off the eye shifts each band's hue a little (the light crosses the film at a slant, so the path
  difference shrinks); a light that swings around the eye keeps it.

## What it does not copy

No real tool, blade or brand. No glow and no blackbody heat colour (the spec: tempering heat is below a visible
glow). No tilt of the bar (a tilt does not shift film hue).
